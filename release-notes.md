Version 10

- Each project's Finance tab has two parts: Client account (contract, invoiced, received, due, still to receive) and AK internal (profit, costs, supplier bills).
- Expenses are supplier bills with item lines, VAT, paid or not, and the supplier's invoice attached.
- A new invoice bills the approved quotation, so advances are deducted automatically. Extra work can be invoiced separately.
- The Windows app tells you when a new version is ready and installs it with one tap.
